Functionality/Performance Improvements and Additions
----------------------------------------------------

- Added the ``skip_objfind`` parameter (``[reduce][[findobj]]``), which skips
  object finding, and the chi-squared statistics saved in the spec2d file, for
  reductions that skip extraction (e.g., IFU data that are combined into
  datacubes).
- Vectorised the construction of the alignment splines used by
  ``pypeit_coadd_datacube`` (:class:`~pypeit.alignframe.AlignmentSplines`).
  The results are identical, and the splines are built ~40 times faster.

Functionality/Performance Improvements and Additions
----------------------------------------------------

- :func:`~pypeit.core.arc.detect_lines` now rejects, by default, lines whose
  Gaussian sigma is less than half the median sigma of the lines in the same
  spectrum (single hot pixels, cosmic rays, noise spikes).  This changes the
  detected arc lines slightly (about 2%, mostly in echelle data) and can change
  the line identifications of the wavelength calibration.  The cut is
  controlled by the new ``[calibrations][wavelengths] min_frac_med_width``
  (0 disables it) and ``min_nlines_med_width`` parameters.  See
  "Under-the-hood Improvements".
- The spectral flexure correction is faster: the FWHM of the archived sky
  spectrum is now measured once per sky file instead of once per slit/object
  (about 8% of a serial Keck/DEIMOS 600ZD reduction).  See "Under-the-hood
  Improvements".


Instrument-specific Updates
---------------------------

- MMT/MMIRS: the ``J_zJ``, ``HK_zJ`` and ``K3000_Kspec`` ``full_template``
  arc templates held three identical copies of the same spectrum.  They now
  hold one.  The copies gave two exactly tied peaks in the global
  cross-correlation, so which copy was used depended on noise at the
  1e-5 level; this put one ``J_zJ`` slit off by a full template copy.
- Gemini/GNIRS echelle (32/mm, SXD): the tilt fit for order 8 now uses
  ``spec_order = 2`` (the other orders keep 4).  Order 8 has few usable lines
  at its blue end, and a 4th-order fit could curl there by tens of pixels
  when the bluest line was lost, making the wavelengths non-monotonic.

Testing
-------

- Added tests of :func:`~pypeit.core.arc.fit_arcspec` (recovery of synthetic
  lines; edge cases: truncated windows, degenerate and NaN windows) and of
  the median-width floor of :func:`~pypeit.core.arc.detect_lines` (it rejects
  narrow spikes but no arc line, is skipped for spectra with few lines, and
  is not applied to the synthetic arc of
  :func:`~pypeit.core.wavecal.wvutils.get_xcorr_arc`).
- Added dev-suite ``vet_tests`` (``test_arcfit.py``) that the lines detected
  in the stored arc spectra of the wavelength calibrations obey the
  median-width floor, and that the Keck/DEIMOS ``830G_L_8100`` solution,
  which sub-resolution spikes can degrade, keeps a low RMS.
- Added a test that the archived sky spectrum FWHM used by the spectral
  flexure correction is measured only once per sky file
  (:func:`~pypeit.core.flexure.get_archive_spectrum`).


Under-the-hood Improvements
---------------------------

- :func:`~pypeit.core.arc.detect_lines` has new ``min_frac_med_width`` and
  ``min_nlines_med_width`` arguments.  By default it rejects lines whose
  Gaussian sigma is less than ``min_frac_med_width`` (0.5) times the median
  sigma of the lines in the same spectrum that pass the other cuts.  Arc lines
  share the instrumental profile, so such features (single hot pixels, cosmic
  rays, noise spikes) are not arc lines; previously there was no lower bound on
  the width, and they were kept whenever ``curve_fit`` converged on them.  The
  cut is skipped for spectra with fewer than ``min_nlines_med_width`` (5)
  lines.  For the wavelength calibration and the tilts, both are set by the new
  ``[calibrations][wavelengths] min_frac_med_width`` and
  ``min_nlines_med_width`` parameters
  (:class:`~pypeit.par.pypeitpar.WavelengthSolutionPar`) and are passed
  explicitly through :mod:`~pypeit.core.wavecal.autoid` and
  :func:`~pypeit.core.tracewave.tilts_find_lines`; other callers use the
  defaults.  The cut is disabled for the callers whose peaks are not arc or sky
  lines (slit edges, object profiles and cross-correlation peaks) and for the
  synthetic arc spectra used for the cross-correlations of the wavelength
  calibration (:func:`~pypeit.core.wavecal.wvutils.get_xcorr_arc`), to which
  the ``full_template`` cross-correlations are very sensitive.  Over the
  development-suite arc spectra it removes 2% of the lines (most strongly in
  echelle data), nearly all outside the final wavelength fits.
- :func:`~pypeit.core.arc.fit_arcspec` no longer raises on fit windows that
  contain non-finite pixels (they are skipped), and fits that return
  non-finite parameters are now flagged with the -999 sentinel instead of
  NaN.
- The spectral FWHM of the archived sky spectrum used by the spectral flexure
  correction (:func:`~pypeit.core.flexure.get_archive_spectrum`) is now
  measured once per sky file and cached, instead of once per slit/object.  The
  results are identical.  In a serial Keck/DEIMOS 600ZD reduction it was
  called 170 times (once per slit and science frame), for ~800 s, which was
  most of the time spent fitting Gaussians to lines.
